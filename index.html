How it works

The complete path from a model on your device to a deployed API.

01 · TRAIN

Train locally with Nova

Use the Nova training site to train your model on your device and export nova-model.json or nova-full-backup.json.

02 · UPLOAD

Import to 0pralx

Sign in, open Create & deploy, and upload your exported Nova model. 0pralx validates the trained weights and tokenizer.

03 · DEPLOY

Run it on the server

Deploy the model on 0pralx infrastructure, test your own model, and create an API key for server-to-server use.
